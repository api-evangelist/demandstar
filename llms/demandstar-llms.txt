# DemandStar (Euna OpenBids)

> DemandStar is a shared supplier network for government eProcurement that has connected local, county, and state government agencies with business suppliers since 1998. Agencies publish bids, RFPs, and solicitations for free; suppliers subscribe to receive automated notifications matched through 9,000+ commodity codes, download bid packages, and submit electronic bid responses. Now part of Euna Solutions and rebranding as Euna OpenBids. DemandStar does not currently publish a public developer API; the links below cover the product and business surfaces.
